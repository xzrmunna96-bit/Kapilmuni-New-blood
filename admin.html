Where does each setting show?

Every card below tells you exactly where on the website/app it appears. Change it here and it updates for everyone instantly.

Website logoHeader · login · landing · ID card · app icon
Support appearanceTop of the Live Support chat
Welcome pop-upRight after a member logs in
Name & addressHeader title · ID card · QR code

1 · Website logo

Shows in:Home header (top-left)Login pageLanding pageSplash screenDonor ID cardCall screenBrowser tab / app icon

The default is a blood-drop logo. Upload your own square logo (PNG with a clear background looks best). Do not use a photo of a person.

2 · Website name & address

Shows in:Home header titleLogin & landing pageDonor ID cardQR verification link

3 · Live Support appearance

Shows in:Top of the Live Support chat window on members' phones

Give your support team a friendly face: a logo or a customer-service photo, a name and a short line. If you leave the picture empty, the website logo is used.

4 · Welcome pop-up

Shows in:Website and app · once, right after a member logs in

5 · Entry pop-ups (image or notice)

Shows in:Home page · shown one by one when a member opens the app

Up to 3. Members close them with ×. Good for offers, camps or announcements with a picture.

6 · ID card signatures

Shows in:Back side of every donor ID card
